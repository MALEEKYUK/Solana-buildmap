What do you want to build on Solana?

Pick a goal and get the open-source repos and docs that fit it, plus a first-week plan.

Why these goals: they follow what people ask for in developer communities such as r/solanadev (checkout flows, how games are built, token and NFT tooling, explorers) and what the Solana Foundation and Anza are actively maintaining on GitHub. Star counts and activity change, so check each repo before you rely on it. Independent project, not affiliated with the Solana Foundation.
